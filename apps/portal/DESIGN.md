# 00AI design refresh

Figma: https://www.figma.com/design/hNYtD63s9cqvvt50U3biW7

The homepage uses a cobalt-and-paper visual system, a Korean typographic hero, an editorial Daejeon case study, accessible step tabs, article source links, service design preview dialogs, and the existing project discovery and publishing flows.

Editable Figma screens: 1440px desktop home, 390px mobile home, AI GOV workspace, Y-HUB workspace. Service preview images are design mockups, labelled accordingly; they are not live data screenshots.
